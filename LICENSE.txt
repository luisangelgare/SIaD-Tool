Purpose

The tool provided in this repository is designed for the identification, analysis, and frequency-domain assessment of power system models using both MATLAB/Simulink and Python/PSCAD environments. It includes custom blocks, scripts, and automated routines to support system identification, stability evaluation, and interaction analysis in modern power systems.
